This application bundles the unmodified lite, single-threaded build from the
stockfish@17.1.0 npm package. Its JavaScript and WASM files are
renamed together to stockfish.js and stockfish.wasm; their contents are unchanged.
Original files: stockfish-17.1-lite-single-03e3232.js, stockfish-17.1-lite-single-03e3232.wasm

A corresponding-source bundle is included beside this notice as:
stockfish-source.tgz
It preserves the original source/build files and includes the lite NNUE network;
only prebuilt JavaScript/WASM outputs are excluded.

Complete upstream source and build instructions at the exact npm gitHead:
https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js/tree/602fd7e1a571a2be71f242942db90483731f2a1f
https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js/archive/602fd7e1a571a2be71f242942db90483731f2a1f.tar.gz

Upstream Stockfish: https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish
NNUE network: https://tests.stockfishchess.org/api/nn/nn-9067e33176e8.nnue

No connection to these services is needed to play or analyze games.
